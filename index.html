About Us

Building With Purpose on Oahu

Ming Yang Construction LLC, known as MY Construction, was founded with a simple belief: your home deserves the same care and attention we'd give our own. Based in Kaneohe on the Windward side of Oahu, our team brings over 30 years of combined experience in construction and carpentry.

Our name, Ming Yang (名扬), means "a name that is recognized" — a reputation built on quality work, honest communication, and lasting results. From detailed finish work to full-scale renovations, we bring backgrounds in residential remodeling and skilled craftsmanship to every project.

What We Do

Our Construction Services

From concept to completion, we deliver quality craftsmanship on every project across Oahu.

Kitchen Remodels

Transform your kitchen into a modern, functional space. We handle cabinetry, countertops, flooring, lighting, plumbing, and every finish detail.

Bathroom Remodels

Full bathroom renovations including tub-to-shower conversions, tile work, mosaic shower floors, vanity installation, and vinyl-wood flooring.

Full Interior Renovation

Complete interior overhauls including walls, flooring, electrical, plumbing, paint, trim, and fixtures. We reimagine your space with care.

Custom Vanities & Countertops

Shower & Tile Work

Expert tile installation, walk-in showers, glass enclosures, mosaic floors, and grab bars. Precision craftsmanship for lasting results.

General Contracting

Whatever your project needs — new builds, extensions, or renovations — we provide comprehensive contracting from permits to punch lists.
